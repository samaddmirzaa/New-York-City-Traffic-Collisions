# NYC Traffic Collision Analysis (2021–2023)

An exploratory data analysis of **238,421 traffic collisions** recorded across New York City's five boroughs between January 2021 and April 2023, using raw NYPD Motor Vehicle Collisions data from NYC Open Data.

The project investigates where collisions are most frequent, when they happen, who is most at risk, what causes them, and which streets are the most dangerous.

---

## Key Findings

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total collisions | 238,421 |
| People killed | 635 |
| People injured | 116,226 |
| Highest-collision borough | Brooklyn (76,416) |
| Most dangerous street | Belt Parkway (3,728 incidents) |
| Leading cause | Driver inattention/distraction |
| Peak collision window | 3 PM – 6 PM on weekdays |

- **Pedestrians** account for the majority of traffic fatalities across the study period.
- **Sedans and station wagons** were the most commonly involved vehicle type.
- Collision rates show a **seasonal pattern**, with warmer months seeing higher volumes.
